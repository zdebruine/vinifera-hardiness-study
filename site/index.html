Cold-Hardy Vinifera Suitability

Two axes — winter survival × ripening attainability — open and reproducible.

Decision support, not validated prediction. Data shown is a small illustrative seed sample, not sourced measurements. See the repository and its caveats.

Suitability explorer

Pick a variety and a site. Survival is screened against the site's coldest seed freeze event; ripening against the site's growing-degree-days. Suitability is the product, with propagated uncertainty.

Full matrix

Every variety × site cell, sortable. Suitability = survival × ripening.

Ripening calculator

P(ripen) = Φ((GDDsite − GDDreq) / σ). The normal CDF gives the logistic-shaped curve. Pick a variety to load its seed requirement, or set values by hand.

Climate & freeze sites

Seed cold-exposed sites with Winkler GDD and the minimum temperature of each recorded freeze event. buried = vines hilled over winter, so survival presence there is uninformative about genetic hardiness.
